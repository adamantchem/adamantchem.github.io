Quality Inspection Across Industries

While our roots are in leather inspection with 30+ years of experience, we've expanded our expertise to serve multiple industries across India's manufacturing landscape. From traditional handicrafts to modern textiles, we ensure your products meet international quality standards.

Leather & Leather Goods

Our flagship expertise. 30+ years inspecting leather products for global buyers.

Products We Inspect:

  • Finished leather (crust, full grain, corrected grain)
  • Leather bags (handbags, travel bags, laptop bags)
  • Small leather goods (wallets, cardholders, key cases)
  • Leather belts and accessories
  • Saddlery and harness products
  • Leather garments and jackets
  • Upholstery and automotive leather

Key Hubs: Chennai, Kolkata, Kanpur, Ambur

Footwear

Comprehensive footwear inspection from raw materials to finished products.

Products We Inspect:

  • Leather shoes (formal, casual, loafers)
  • Sandals and chappals
  • Ladies footwear
  • Sports and athletic footwear
  • Safety footwear
  • Ethnic footwear (juttis, mojaris)
  • Shoe components (uppers, soles, insoles)

Key Hubs: Agra, Kanpur, Chennai

Textiles & Apparel

  • Fashion accessories
  • Uniforms and workwear
