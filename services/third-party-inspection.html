Third Party Inspection Services in India

As an independent third-party inspection agency, we provide unbiased quality verification for importers and merchandisers sourcing products from India.

What is Third Party Inspection?

Third-party inspection involves an independent quality control company (like Adamant Inspection Services) verifying product quality on behalf of buyers. This ensures objective assessment without conflict of interest from manufacturers.

Types of Third Party Inspections

  • Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI): Final check when goods are 80-100% packed
  • During Production Inspection (DUPRO): Check when 20-60% of production is complete
  • Initial Production Check (IPC): Verify raw materials and early production
  • Container Loading Supervision: Verify loading and sealing
  • Random Vendor Inspection: Unannounced factory visits

Products We Inspect

  • Finished leather and wet blue
  • Leather goods (bags, belts, wallets)
  • Footwear (leather and synthetic)
  • Textiles and apparel
  • Handicrafts and home decor
  • Metal products (Moradabad brassware)
  • Carpets and rugs
  • Home furnishings

Our Inspection Report Includes

  1. Product conformity to specifications
  2. Workmanship and finish quality
  3. Quantity verification
  4. Packaging and labeling check
  5. Defect classification (critical, major, minor)
  6. Photo documentation
  7. Pass/Fail recommendation

Coverage Areas
