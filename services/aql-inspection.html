AQL Inspection Services in India

Acceptance Quality Limit (AQL) inspection is the cornerstone of quality control for manufactured goods. Our certified inspectors ensure your products meet international standards before shipment.

What is AQL Inspection?

AQL (Acceptance Quality Limit) is a statistical sampling method defined by ISO 2859-1 that determines the maximum acceptable number of defective units in a random sample. It's the global standard for pre-shipment quality control.

Our AQL Inspection Process

  1. Sample Selection: Random sample drawn per ISO 2859-1 tables
  2. Visual Inspection: Check for cosmetic defects, color consistency, stitching
  3. Measurement Verification: Dimensions, weight, specifications
  4. Functionality Testing: Zippers, buckles, closures, durability
  5. Packaging Check: Labels, barcodes, packaging integrity
  6. Detailed Report: Photo documentation with pass/fail results
