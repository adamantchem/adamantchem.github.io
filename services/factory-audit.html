Factory Audit Services in India

Comprehensive factory and supplier audits to verify manufacturing capabilities, compliance standards, and quality systems before you commit to a supplier.

Types of Factory Audits

1. Capability & Capacity Audit

Assess a factory's ability to meet your production requirements:

  • Production capacity analysis
  • Machinery and equipment assessment
  • Workforce evaluation
  • Lead time verification
  • Past performance review

2. Quality System Audit

  • Quality management procedures
  • Incoming material inspection
  • In-line quality checks
  • Final inspection protocols
  • Documentation and traceability

3. Social Compliance Audit

Verify ethical manufacturing practices:

  • Labor conditions and working hours
  • Health and safety compliance
  • Child labor verification
  • Fair wage practices
  • Environmental compliance

4. Technical Audit

  • Manufacturing process evaluation
  • Technical capability assessment
  • R&D capabilities
  • Sample development ability

Audit Checklist Includes

  • Legal documentation
  • Factory layout and cleanliness
  • Production workflow
  • Quality control systems
  • Raw material storage
  • Machinery condition
  • Worker skills and training
  • Safety measures
  • Packaging and shipping area
  • Previous client references

Audit Report

Our detailed audit report includes photographs, scoring matrix, risk assessment, and actionable recommendations.

Coverage

Factory audits available across India: Chennai, Kolkata, Kanpur, Delhi, Mumbai, Moradabad, Jaipur, and all manufacturing hubs.
